Vault — KV operations + local AES-256-GCM crypto (Cat 3, H18).

Wire format for LocalEncrypt (matches workos-go): LEB128(len(encryptedKeys)) || encryptedKeys || nonce(12) || ciphertext+tag the whole thing then base64-encoded.
